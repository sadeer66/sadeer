FurniPlan Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

FurniPlan is designed to work locally on your device. We respect your privacy and do not collect, sell, or share personal data.

Data Collection

FurniPlan does not collect personal information, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, location data, contacts, photos, or other user data from your device.

Project Files

Floor plans, furniture layouts, project information, exported PNG files, and exported PDF files are created and stored locally on your device unless you choose to share or save them elsewhere using system sharing features.

Third-Party Tracking

FurniPlan does not use advertising networks or third-party tracking services.

Children's Privacy

FurniPlan does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the app's privacy practices change.
